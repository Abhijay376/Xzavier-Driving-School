Philippines to VicRoads Driving Licence Conversion

How to convert your Philippines driving licence to a Victorian licence: your tier, requirements, and step-by-step process.

Reviewed by Medii Sha, VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructor
105+ 5-Star Google Reviews VicRoads ADI Accredited 7+ Years on Dandenong Roads Dual-Control Test Cars Available

If you’re moving to Victoria with a Philippines driving licence and want to convert overseas licence Victoria requirements into a plain checklist, you’re in the right place. This guide covers exactly what a Philippines licence holder needs to do to convert their licence to a Victorian one, and it forms part of our broader guide to convert international licence Melbourne newcomers can follow for any country.

Your Tier: Experienced Driver Recognition Country

A knowledge test is generally required, and a practical driving test as well if you're under 25 or have less driving experience.

Philippine driving licences fall under Victoria’s Experienced Driver Recognition tier. Requirements depend on your age and years of driving experience on your Philippine licence.

Age / Experience Requirement

Under 25 usually needs a knowledge + practical test; 25+ with 3+ years’ experience may qualify for a more direct pathway.

Step-by-Step: Philippines Licence to Victorian Licence

  1. Confirm your tier

    Use our checker to confirm your Philippines licence falls under the Experienced Driver Recognition Country tier before starting the process.

  2. Gather your documents

    Valid Philippines licence, passport/visa, proof of Victorian address, and an official English translation if your licence isn’t in English.

  3. Book any required tests

    A knowledge test is generally required, and a practical driving test as well if you're under 25 or have less driving experience.

  4. Prepare with a local instructor

    If a practical test is required, XDS instructors can prepare you for your specific VicRoads test centre. See our guides for Frankston, Mooroolbark, Pakenham and Heatherton.

  5. Receive your Victorian licence

    Once you’ve completed the required steps, your Victorian driver licence is issued and your Philippines licence is typically surrendered.
