United Kingdom to VicRoads Driving Licence Conversion

How to convert your United Kingdom driving licence to a Victorian licence — your tier, requirements, and step-by-step process.

Reviewed by Medii Sha, VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructor

If you’re moving to Victoria with a United Kingdom driving licence and want to convert overseas licence Victoria requirements into a plain checklist, you’re in the right place. This guide covers exactly what a United Kingdom licence holder needs to do to convert their licence to a Victorian one — part of our broader guide to convert international licence Melbourne newcomers can follow for any country.

Your Tier: Recognised Country

Direct exchange — no knowledge or practical test required in most cases.

UK driving licences are on Victoria’s Recognised country list, meaning a direct exchange is usually possible with no test required.

Age / Experience Requirement

No age or experience requirement in most cases.

Step-by-Step: United Kingdom Licence to Victorian Licence

  1. Confirm your tier

    Use our checker to confirm your United Kingdom licence falls under the Recognised Country tier before starting the process.

  2. Gather your documents

    Valid United Kingdom licence, passport/visa, proof of Victorian address, and an official English translation if your licence isn’t in English.

  3. Book any required tests

    Direct exchange — no knowledge or practical test required in most cases.

  4. Prepare with a local instructor

    If a practical test is required, XDS instructors can prepare you for your specific VicRoads test centre — see our guides for Frankston, Mooroolbark, Pakenham and Heatherton.

  5. Receive your Victorian licence

    Once you’ve completed the required steps, your Victorian driver licence is issued and your United Kingdom licence is typically surrendered.
