United Kingdom to VicRoads Driving Licence Conversion

How to convert your United Kingdom driving licence to a Victorian licence: your tier, requirements, and step-by-step process.

Reviewed by Medii Sha, VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructor
105+ 5-Star Google Reviews VicRoads ADI Accredited 7+ Years on Dandenong Roads Dual-Control Test Cars Available

If you plan to live in Victoria for more than 6 months, VicRoads says you must convert your overseas licence to a Victorian one. The 6 months starts from the day you first begin living in Victoria. Here is what that means for someone holding a United Kingdom licence.

Things United Kingdom Licence Holders Should Know

  • Driving side: The UK drives on the left, and most road rules are similar. Speeds are in km/h, and there are some rules that catch UK drivers out, like hook turns in Melbourne’s CBD.
  • Translation: UK licences are in English, so no translation is needed. A UK provisional licence is a learner licence, not a full licence, so it does not qualify for a direct swap.

Habits we work on with United Kingdom drivers

  • Remembering speeds are in km/h, not mph
  • Giving way at roundabouts to vehicles already on them, which works the same as in the UK
  • Hook turns at signed intersections in the Melbourne CBD
  • 40 km/h school zones at set times

Good to know

  • VicRoads lets you take the hazard perception test online, and has a free practice version.
  • Permanent residents may be able to get documents translated free through the Department of Home Affairs Free Translating Service.
  • VicRoads test and licence fees are paid separately to any lessons.

Rules change. Always confirm your exact requirements with the VicRoads requirements checker before you book anything.

Your Tier: Recognised Country

Direct exchange, with no knowledge or practical test required in most cases.

UK licences are on Victoria’s recognised country list, so a full UK car licence can usually be swapped for a Victorian licence without a knowledge or drive test.

Does age or experience change this?

No. You need a current full licence (not a learner or provisional licence), but age and years of experience do not change the requirement.

Step-by-Step: United Kingdom Licence to Victorian Licence

  1. Check with VicRoads

    Run your details through the VicRoads requirements checker to confirm what applies to your United Kingdom licence.

  2. Gather your documents

    Valid United Kingdom licence, passport/visa, proof of Victorian address, and an official English translation if your licence isn’t in English.

  3. Book any required tests

    Direct exchange, with no knowledge or practical test required in most cases.

  4. Prepare with a local instructor

    If a practical test is required, XDS instructors can prepare you for your specific VicRoads test centre. See our guides for Frankston, Ringwood, Pakenham and Heatherton.

  5. Receive your Victorian licence

    Once you’ve completed the required steps, your Victorian driver licence is issued and your United Kingdom licence is typically surrendered.
