VicRoads Drive Test Pakenham

Test centre details, the two-stage test format, and the local roads XDS instructors use to prepare Pakenham learners.

Reviewed by Medii Sha, VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructor
7+ Years Experience Test-Day Car Options Flexible Instructor Options VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructors

Test Centre Details

Location3/4 Stephenson St, Pakenham VIC 3810
HoursMonday to Friday, 8:30 am – 4:30 pm (by appointment only)
Public transit accessClose to Pakenham railway station (Pakenham line) and the Pakenham bus interchange on Station Street

Quick facts

  • Minor faults: up to 15 allowed before failing
  • Two stages: 10 minutes + 20 minutes
  • Pre-drive safety check required before you start driving
  • One of the busier growth-corridor test centres — book appointments early

Test Format & Rules

The same statewide VicRoads structure applies at every test centre, including Pakenham.

Stage 1 — Basic Controls

Duration: 10 minutes

Low-risk manoeuvres such as a 3-point turn or reverse parallel park, usually on the quieter streets near the centre.

Stage 2 — Busy Traffic

Duration: 20 minutes

Busier roads around Pakenham, including roundabouts, lane changes and local traffic.

Immediate Termination Errors

  • Intervention by the tester
  • Disobeying a direction/sign
  • Collision
  • Failing to give way
  • Excessive speed
  • Stopping in a dangerous position
  • Failing to stop
  • Dangerous action

Critical Errors

More than 1 in Stage 1, or more than 2 overall, ends the test.

  • Too slow
  • Fail to look
  • Fail to signal
  • Blocking a pedestrian crossing
  • Mounting the kerb
  • Stalling
  • Incomplete stop
  • Illegal action

Local Pakenham Test Roads & Fail Points

Guidance based on instructor experience training students for this test centre.

1

Princes Highway 80 km/h

High-speed merging and lane discipline through Pakenham’s main highway corridor.

2

John Street & Main Street 50 km/h

Busy town-centre streets with pedestrian crossings and frequent give-way situations.

3

Toomuc Valley Road roundabouts 50 / 60 km/h

Roundabout entry and exit lane discipline is a common focus area in Stage 2.

Vehicle Requirements

Using your own vehicle

  • Roadworthy and currently registered
  • Working horn, indicators, brake lights, headlights, wipers
  • Functioning front and rear demisters
  • Valid insurance and correctly displayed L-plates

Using an XDS instructor vehicle

  • Already meets all VicRoads compliance requirements
  • Dual-control for added safety
  • Included in the Express Test Package and Ultimate Test Pass Pack
