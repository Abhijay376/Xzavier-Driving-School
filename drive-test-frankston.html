VicRoads Drive Test Frankston

Everything you need to know about the Frankston (Seaford) VicRoads test centre — local roads, common fail points, and how XDS instructors prepare you for this specific route.

Reviewed by Medii Sha, VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructor
7+ Years Experience Test-Day Car Options Flexible Instructor Options VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructors

Test Centre Details

Location71 Hartnett Dr, Seaford VIC 3198
HoursMonday to Friday, 8:30 am – 4:30 pm (by appointment only)
Public transit accessPeninsula Bvd / Hartnett Dr bus stop (4 min walk) · Lorna St / Klauer St bus stop (6 min walk) · Bus routes 779 & 901 · Frankston train line

Quick facts

  • Minor faults: up to 15 allowed before failing
  • Test runs in two stages: 10 minutes + 20 minutes
  • Pre-drive safety check required before you start driving
  • Test vehicle must be roadworthy and compliant

Test Format & Rules

Minor vs. Major / Dangerous Faults

  • Minor faults: up to 15 allowed across the whole test
  • 16th minor fault results in an automatic fail
  • Major or dangerous faults result in an immediate fail, regardless of minor fault count

Pre-Drive Safety Checks Checklist

  • Horn
  • Indicators
  • Wipers
  • Brake lights
  • Demisters (front & rear)
  • Boot
  • Ignition in neutral / park before starting

Stage 1 — Basic Controls

Duration: 10 minutes

Low-risk environment covering basic vehicle control and low-speed manoeuvres such as a 3-point turn or reverse parallel park. You must pass Stage 1 before progressing to Stage 2.

Stage 2 — Busy Traffic

Duration: 20 minutes

Conducted in busier traffic conditions: high-speed merging, lane changes, and complex intersections around Frankston and Seaford.

Immediate Termination Errors

  • Intervention by the tester
  • Disobeying a direction/sign
  • Collision
  • Failing to give way
  • Excessive speed
  • Stopping in a dangerous position
  • Failing to stop
  • Dangerous action

Critical Errors

More than 1 in Stage 1, or more than 2 overall, ends the test.

  • Too slow
  • Fail to look
  • Fail to signal
  • Blocking a pedestrian crossing
  • Mounting the kerb
  • Stalling
  • Incomplete stop
  • Illegal action

Local Frankston Test Roads & Fail Points

Real fail-point knowledge from instructors who test students on this exact route.

1

Hartnett Drive 60 km/h

Immediate roundabout negotiation right as you exit the VicRoads driveway — a common early hesitation point.

2

Frankston-Dandenong Road 80 km/h

Requires confident high-speed gap selection and lane switching in fast-moving traffic.

3

Klauer Street & Seaford Road 60 / 40 km/h

School zone speed limit timing traps — a frequent source of minor and critical speed faults.

4

Galway Street & Hadley Street 40 / 50 km/h

Narrow residential streets commonly used for Stage 1 low-speed manoeuvres.

Vehicle Requirements

Using your own vehicle

  • Roadworthy and currently registered
  • Working horn, indicators, brake lights, headlights, wipers
  • Functioning front and rear demisters
  • Valid insurance and correctly displayed L-plates

Using an XDS instructor vehicle

  • Already meets all VicRoads compliance requirements
  • Dual-control for added safety
  • Included in the Express Test Package and Ultimate Test Pass Pack
