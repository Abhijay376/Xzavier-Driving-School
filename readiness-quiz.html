How to Pass Your VicRoads Driving Test

Straightforward, instructor-reviewed guidance for learners preparing for their VicRoads drive test in Melbourne.

Common VicRoads Test Fail Reasons

Most learners who don’t pass fall down on a small number of repeat issues: incomplete mirror and blind-spot checks, hesitating at roundabouts, poor speed control through school zones, and struggling with parallel or reverse parking under time pressure. The readiness quiz above is built around these exact weak spots so you can target your remaining practice instead of guessing.

How Many Lessons Before My Driving Test?

There’s no single answer — it depends on your existing supervised driving hours and confidence level. As a general guide, most learners with solid practice need 5–10 lessons focused on test-day manoeuvres and their local test route; learners with less experience or specific weak areas often benefit from a full lesson package such as our 5-Lesson Pass or Ultimate Test Pass Pack. Your quiz result above gives a personalised starting point.

Driving Test Practice Tips for Melbourne Learners

  • Practise on or near your actual test route before test day — see our test centre guides for Frankston, Mooroolbark, Pakenham and Heatherton.
  • Do a full pre-drive safety check every time you practise, not just before the test.
  • Book a lesson in the same time slot as your test so you’re used to that traffic pattern.
  • Focus extra practice on whatever the quiz above flags as your weak spot — don’t just repeat what you’re already good at.
