Driving Lessons in Carrum Downs

Driving lessons in Carrum Downs with pickup from home, school or work, and test preparation for VicRoads Frankston.

Reviewed by Medii Sha, VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructor

Carrum Downs is one of the closest suburbs to the VicRoads test centre in Seaford. It has a large industrial area, so learners here get used to sharing the road with trucks early on.

What You Will Practise in Carrum Downs

  • Frankston-Dandenong Road, where trucks and fast traffic share the lanes
  • Hall Road and the roundabouts near the shopping centre
  • EastLink entry and exit ramps on the edge of the suburb

Nearest VicRoads Test Centre

Most Carrum Downs learners sit their test at VicRoads Frankston (71 Hartnett Dr, Seaford VIC 3198), roughly 2 km away in a straight line.

Our Tip for Carrum Downs Learners

We teach you how to give trucks the space they need, especially when they turn. Driving calmly around heavy vehicles is a skill testers pay attention to.

Lessons and Prices for Carrum Downs Learners

All our lessons are automatic, in a dual-control car, and focused on one thing: getting you through your VicRoads test. Our students have a 99% pass rate.

  • Single lesson: $70, with pickup in Carrum Downs.
  • 5-Lesson Pass: $325.
  • Express Test Package: $220, including use of our dual-control car for your test at VicRoads Frankston.
  • Ultimate Test Pass Pack: $560.

If you are under 21, VicRoads needs you to log 120 hours of supervised driving, including 20 hours at night, before your drive test. Lessons with us count towards those hours. VicRoads test and booking fees are paid separately. See full package details.
